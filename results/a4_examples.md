# A4 examples: drafts of the Phase 8 evaluation

Excerpts of the defense drafts behind the main findings of Phase 8 (`PHASE_8_SUMMARY.md`). Each draft is named by its test notice, drafting mode and run (e.g. N14 full, run 1); the files are in `data/v2/a4/drafts/` (v2.6) and `data/v2/a4_v27/drafts/` (v2.7). Quotations are in Portuguese with a free English translation. *Translations are drafts, to be reviewed by the author* (ground rule 10). Bold in a quotation is the draft's own.

## 1. A ground taken from the precedents, stated as a fact of the case (v2.6), and as a premise to verify (v2.7)

Many precedents supplied with the test notices excluded an increase of the fine for past offences because the record of those offences was not in the initial notice (art. 100, VII, of Resolução CNSP 243/2011). In v2.6 the drafts transferred that ground to their own case and asserted its factual premise, which no test notice states. The claim appears in 62% to 75% of the v2.6 drafts of every mode given precedents, in none of the 45 drafts without precedents, and in none of the 15 real appeals.

**N14, full, run 1, v2.6:**

> A autoridade julgadora de origem procedeu à majoração da penalidade fundamentando-se em relatório de antecedentes que **não constava da representação inicial**, tendo sido acostado ao feito em momento posterior à apresentação da defesa administrativa da Recorrente.

*The first-instance authority increased the penalty on the basis of a record of past offences that **was not in the initial notice**, having been added to the file after the appellant filed its administrative defence.*

N14's case document says only that the fine was increased for past offences (R$ 9.000,00). It says nothing about when the record was added.

**The same notice, mode and run, v2.7:**

> a) A **exclusão integral da majoração de R$ 9.000,00 aplicada a título de antecedentes**, [VERIFICAR: se não informados na Representação inaugural ou se o relatório foi acostado após a defesa prévia], com fundamento no artigo 100, inciso VII, da Resolução CNSP nº 243/2011;

*a) The **full exclusion of the R$ 9.000,00 increase applied for past offences**, [TO VERIFY: whether they were not reported in the initial notice or the record was added after the first defence], under article 100, item VII, of Resolução CNSP 243/2011;*

The ground is kept as a request; its premise is marked for the lawyer to confirm in the records. The same pattern in N13, full, run 1: v2.6 asserts "o relatório de antecedentes não acompanhou a peça inicial acusatória" (*the record of past offences did not accompany the initial charge*); v2.7 writes "Se comprovada a juntada tardia do relatório de antecedentes nos autos [VERIFICAR: nos autos]" (*If the late filing of the record is proven [TO VERIFY: in the records]*).

In the author's blind audits, two of the clearest v2.6 inventions were this claim: "relatório juntado aos autos após a apresentação da defesa administrativa inaugural" (N08, no legal search, run 3; *record added to the file after the first defence*) and "sem que tais circunstâncias tivessem sido pormenorizadas, individualizadas e formalmente imputadas à Recorrente" (N06, full, run 1; *without those circumstances being detailed, individualised and formally charged to the appellant*). No v2.7 passage of his audit asserts it.

## 2. Other invented case facts

**v2.6.** A cause for a failed data upload: "divergências de validação e inconsistências de parametrização" (N14, full, run 2; *validation discrepancies and parametrisation inconsistencies*); the notice says only that the first valid upload came late. An origin story for an association: founded because of the "recusa ou das tarifas impeditivas praticadas pelo mercado segurador comercial" (N12, no legal search, run 1; *refusal, or prohibitive rates, of the commercial insurance market*).

**v2.7, the two invented passages of the author's audit, both weak.** "muito antes do encerramento das datas de consolidação e publicação das demonstrações de balanço" (N01, no template, run 1; *long before the closing and publication dates of the financial statements*): a premise from the regulatory calendar, presented as established. "compromete o patrimônio de dezenas de famílias de trabalhadores associados" (N12, no legal search, run 2; *endangers the assets of dozens of families of member workers*): a membership size the notice does not give.

## 3. A real precedent cited under a wrong ruling number (v2.6)

**N05, no template, run 3, v2.6:**

> **Acórdão CRSNSP 286/2021 (Processo nº 15414.626623/2017-79 – Rel. Cons. Ronaldo Guimarães Gallo):** *"RECURSO ADMINISTRATIVO. Contrato de seguro. Furto. Perda total do veículo. Exigência de quitação do IPVA. Responsabilidade do segurado. Recurso conhecido e provido."*

*Ruling CRSNSP 286/2021 (Process 15414.626623/2017-79, rapporteur Ronaldo Guimarães Gallo): "ADMINISTRATIVE APPEAL. Insurance contract. Theft. Total loss of the vehicle. Requirement to have paid the vehicle tax. Responsibility of the insured. Appeal admitted and granted."*

The process is a supplied precedent and the headnote is its own, but its ruling is ACÓRDÃO CRSNSP 7357/2021; 286/2021 is no CRSNSP ruling (the number of a SUSEP opinion appears in another decision's report). Three v2.6 drafts and one v2.7 draft cite supplied precedents under a wrong ruling number.

## 4. A ruling that looks invented, with a quoted headnote (v2.7)

**N05, no precedents, run 1, v2.7:**

> consoante expressa a diretriz do Acórdão CRSNSP nº 5.812/2018: *"A dosimetria da sanção pecuniária deve respeitar estritamente os princípios da razoabilidade e proporcionalidade, considerando a primariedade da entidade, a boa-fé e a ausência de prejuízo efetivo ou sistêmico ao mercado e aos segurados."*

*as the guideline of Ruling CRSNSP 5.812/2018 states: "The setting of a fine must strictly respect the principles of reasonableness and proportionality, considering the entity's first offence, its good faith and the absence of actual or systemic harm to the market and the insured."*

This draft was given no precedents. No decision of the corpus carries that ruling number, and the quoted headnote reads as generic. Not verified against the CRSNSP's full records. The fact controls of v2.7 act on facts of the case, not on citations.

## 5. A draft that found its own source decision through the web search (v2.6)

**N11, no template, run 2, v2.6:**

> **Acórdão CRSNSP nº 6743/2020 (Processo nº 15414.621101/2018-61) e Acórdão CRSNSP nº 555/2024 (Processo nº 15414.633391/2018-96)**: Consagram o entendimento de que a imputação de solidariedade com base no § 2º do art. 4º da Resolução CNSP nº 243/2011 transborda a competência regulamentar [...]

*Ruling CRSNSP 6743/2020 (Process 15414.621101/2018-61) and Ruling CRSNSP 555/2024 (Process 15414.633391/2018-96): they establish that imposing joint liability under § 2 of art. 4 of Resolução CNSP 243/2011 exceeds the regulatory competence [...]*

Process 15414.633391/2018-96 is N11's own source decision, which the board decided on this very ground (no joint liability of the director). The precedents supplied exclude it, so the legal search (web) found it. One draft of 213 in v2.6, none in v2.7. Ruling 6743/2020 is not in the corpus (probably found by the same search; not verified).
